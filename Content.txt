Do NOT treat the "Response Required" options (e.g. "Yes / No / Unable to Confirm") as a 
list you are classifying the answer into. You are extracting the LITERAL, VERBATIM text 
that Trinity typed into the "Trinity Response / Details" cell — copy it exactly as written, 
in full, including every word. Do not truncate, paraphrase, auto-complete, or round off to 
the closest matching option. If Trinity typed "Unable to Confirm at this time", extract the 
full phrase "Unable to Confirm at this time" — not just "Unable to Confirm" or "Unable to".


A single response value may be split across multiple HTML tags, spans, or line-wrapped 
elements even mid-word or mid-phrase (e.g. "Unable to" in one element and "Confirm" in 
the next). Always read and concatenate ALL text content within a table cell (<td>) — from 
its opening tag to its closing tag — regardless of how many nested tags or line breaks 
appear inside it. Never stop at the first inner tag boundary.
